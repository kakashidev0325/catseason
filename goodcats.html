House rules

Five lines.
Then stop.

  1. 01
    Stay in the pile

    The joke is that everyone is touching. Cropping one cat out and calling it a brand is how the season ends.

  2. 02
    Post the picture

    Steal the stills. Repost them. Print them on something soft. The best new ones get pinned.

  3. 03
    Make no promises

    No dates, no utility, no “soon.” Cat Season is a meme. If a post sounds like a plan, it is the wrong post.

  4. 04
    Brighter days, out loud

    Goodcats is the mood, not a membership. Be decent in the chat. The pile is crowded enough.

  5. 05
    Only what you can lose

    $CATSZN has no intrinsic value and no official team. Do your own research. Never jump in more than you can lose.
